Leda Kaberidi

Leda Kaberidi (Greek: Λήδα Καμπερίδη, born 04 May 2004) is a filmmaker, editor from Greece. She graduated from the Athens Music School. She has studied Film Production at SAE Creative Media Education. She also currently studies Industrial Management and Technology at the University of Piraeus and attending the course of YAFKA School for VFX composing.
